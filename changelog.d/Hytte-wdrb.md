- **Kids' Wardrobe** - New feature (flag `wardrobe`, default off) for tracking children's clothes, shoes and gear. Kid profiles are standalone rows (no Google account needed) with append-only height/foot-length measurement history. Nordic clothing sizes (height in cm) and EU shoe sizes are derived automatically — the page shows both "fits now" and "buy" sizes, plus growth rate per month once two measurements span 14+ days. Sixteen default categories (Norwegian barnehage packing list: regntøy, ullundertøy, skiftetøy, ...) are seeded per user on first use with editable target quantities; anything below target, or flagged "too small", appears in a computed Needs tab with the recommended replacement size. Items carry size, quantity, condition, status (in use / too small / stored as hand-me-down), location (home / kindergarten / school / cabin) and season. (Hytte-wdrb)
