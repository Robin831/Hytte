category: Changed
- **Pokémon catalogue from TCGdex** - Sets, cards, images and prices now come from TCGdex instead of pokemontcg.io, which has been deprecated (it moved to the paid Scrydex API and goes offline on 1 March 2027) and stopped getting new sets. Existing cards keep their ids, so collections and scans are untouched; new sets such as Delta Reign appear again. Prices are the card's own Cardmarket price (plain print and reverse holo), which corrects cards that were valued at a special print's price (e.g. a €0.06 Ho-Oh shown at €73). The weekly sync refreshes every card; the daily price refresh covers owned cards.
