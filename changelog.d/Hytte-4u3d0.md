category: Added
- **Response types and validator for the 26-week macro plan** - New `internal/stride/macro_types.go` defines `MacroPlanResponse` and `MacroWeekResponse`, the Go shape of the JSON the macro coach returns, mirroring the output contract in `macro_prompt.go` key for key and reusing the existing `MacroGoal`, `Mesocycle` and `KeySession` types where the persisted and returned shapes are identical. New `internal/stride/macro_validate.go` adds `ValidateMacroPlan`, which checks a returned block against the horizon it was asked for: exactly 26 weeks, contiguous Mondays numbered 1-26 from the block start, phase and load_level drawn from the allowed enums, every week naming a mesocycle that exists, mesocycles tiling the block with no gaps or overlaps, no week above the athlete's weekly distance cap, no week-over-week volume jump above +10% except directly after a deload, an A-priority half marathon or longer owning its week as a race week with the two weeks before it tapering, no taper week spent on a 5 km or 10 km race, and every week's `race_id` naming one of the races that week contains, with no week naming a race the block does not contain. The race rules cope with awkward calendars: two A races within two weeks of each other do not require the same week to be both a race week and a taper week, a week holding two races is satisfied by either of their ids (the single `race_id` field cannot name both), and the closing weeks may taper for an A race that sits just past the block's horizon without the short-race rule flagging them. All the violations found in one pass are reported together as a single human-readable error naming the offending week and the expected value, so the message can be handed straight back to the model on a retry. Nothing calls the validator yet — the generation path that runs it before persisting lands separately. (Hytte-4u3d0)
