category: Added
- **Plan queue on the Suggestions page** - "Plan it" can now be clicked on several suggestions in a row: each click instantly moves the card out of the pending list and into a new plan-queue panel above the tabs, and the plans run strictly one at a time. The panel shows which suggestion is being planned right now, how many are waiting (each removable from the queue), failures with the server's error and a retry button, and how many plans finished this session. The queue keeps draining while navigating elsewhere in the app; failed suggestions drop back into the pending list.
