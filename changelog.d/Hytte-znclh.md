- **Import a recipe from a URL** - `POST /api/recipes/import` fetches a recipe page, strips it to text (keeping any schema.org JSON-LD block) and asks Claude to return a structured recipe: title, notes, servings, ingredients with quantity/unit/name/raw line, and steps with optional durations. The endpoint is read-only — it returns the parsed recipe for you to edit and never writes to the database, so a failed fetch or an unparseable page can't leave a half-imported recipe behind. Fetches are capped at 15s and 2 MiB and refuse to reach internal addresses. (Hytte-znclh)
