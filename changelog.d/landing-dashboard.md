category: Changed
- **The home page is now your dashboard** - Signing in lands on the dashboard itself instead of a clock with a link to it; `/dashboard` and the old `/today` page redirect there, and the separate Dashboard menu item is gone. Signed-out visitors get a welcome with a sign-in button and the weather. The greeting shrank to a one-line header.
- **Nine new dashboard widgets** - Work hours (punch in and out right from the page), Trips (ongoing or next trip with a countdown), Races (your next race and nearest deadlines), Stride (today's session), Family chat (unread messages), Wordfeud (games waiting on you), Stars, Chores (a child's chores, or a parent's approvals) and Homework. Each shows only for users with that feature; children get their own things first by default and can rearrange the grid like everyone else.
