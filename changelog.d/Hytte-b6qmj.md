- **Kiosk night mode and burn-in pixel shift** - The kiosk screen now dims to a reduced-contrast palette outside daylight hours, driven by the payload's sunrise/sunset (including polar day/night) and any `dim` / `dim_start` / `dim_end` overrides on the kiosk token; every panel dims together (clock, departures, weather and sunrise strips). The whole layout is also nudged a few pixels every few minutes to spread panel wear. Both ride the existing clock tick, so nothing reloads. A dim window that is half-configured or unparsable is dropped server-side and logged, so the fallback to the sun window is no longer silent. (Hytte-b6qmj)
