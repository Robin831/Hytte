- **A warmup can no longer be smeared into a rep and reported as a sustained effort** - The predictor's "best clean sustained efforts" list rejected reps+recovery blocks by pace spread, but that test compares the two extreme laps and ignores how long each lasted, so a 15-minute warmup joined to one 6-minute rep slipped through at 10.8% spread and the headline fact read "4.1 km in 21:00 at 5:08/km" — a pace nobody ran, 71% warmup by the clock, understating the athlete. Two gates now enforce that a window is one effort: its pace must stay within 6% of its own fastest lap (duration-weighted, and HR-free so it works without a strap), and no lap-to-lap transition may get materially faster and materially higher-HR at the same time. Both are step tests rather than range tests, so honest easy runs whose HR drifts 121-151 over the run, or ramps 19 bpm across the opening kilometre at unchanged pace, are untouched. The predicted times themselves do not move — that anchor was already derived from the interval work laps. The facts block also now states plainly that these windows are what was run, not maximal efforts. (Hytte-8zdll)
