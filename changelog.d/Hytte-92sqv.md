- **Indoor sessions now count in the race prediction via recorded belt speed** - For a mostly-indoor athlete the predictor was effectively an n=1 estimate off the rare outdoor session. Indoor work clusters (already HR-selected) now gain an outdoor-equivalent pace from the belt speed the athlete actually recorded — the structured speed plan's interval segments first, the prescribed belt speed parsed from that day's Stride session as fallback (the athlete follows prescriptions precisely), skipped entirely when neither exists — multiplied by the calibration factor parsed from the treadmill-calibration setting ("x1.03" / "3%", conservative 1.0 default). Converted efforts join the deterministic anchor under the same HR gating as outdoor work; watch pace remains banned everywhere, and free-text feel notes are deliberately not parsed (formats vary and a recorded belt/plate swap changed the speed scale mid-history).
