- **Livestream: notifications, replays, share links, live map** - Going live can notify family by push (on by default, respects quiet hours), and can optionally be recorded (off by default): the replay appears under Live → Replays as a seekable MP4, linked to the matching workout. A "Share link" lets people without an account watch via `/watch/<token>` until the stream ends. With "Share location" on, viewers get a live route map with distance and pace, and the replay shows the route with a marker that follows the video. Viewers now see the phone's battery level and a "Back in a moment…" frame during reconnects, and the phone drops to low-data mode by itself when the signal is weak. (Hytte-krwxy)
