- **Rain chance and wind gusts on the weather page** - The forecast now comes from MET's `complete` product instead of `compact`, so it carries precipitation probability and gust speed. Rain chance appears under each hour in the hourly strip, in the hourly chart's tooltip, and as the day's maximum in every 7-day row; the current-conditions card shows gusts when they exceed the steady wind by at least 2 m/s. Hours where MET omits a probability render exactly as before. Upstream request volume is unchanged — the 30-minute cache and singleflight group are untouched. (Hytte-pglng)
