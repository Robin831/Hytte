- **Stride macro plan persistence** - New `stride_macro_plans`, `stride_macro_weeks` and `stride_goal_revisions` tables plus a Go store for the long-horizon (26-week) training block: goal, mesocycle periodisation and one row per week, with an append-only goal history so the block's target can be adjusted weekly and audited. AI-authored content (goal, periodisation, prompt/response, key sessions, week intent, revision reasons) is encrypted at rest; the dates, phase, load level, status and volume targets stay queryable. `stride_plans` gains `macro_week_id` (link back to the macro week a weekly plan materialises) and an encrypted `adjustment_summary`. Schema and store only — nothing generates or reads macro plans yet. (Hytte-ifjr2.1)
