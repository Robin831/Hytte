category: Fixed
- **Race predictor reads treadmill sessions by heart rate instead of pace** - Indoor work-lap clustering keyed on the watch's pace field, which on a treadmill tracks cadence rather than belt speed. The ordering does not just blur, it inverts: a belt-9.8 warmup reads *faster* than a belt-12.0 threshold rep, so the 8% pace band anchored on the warmup and threw the reps out. In practice the 3x10min threshold session was discarded entirely while two easy treadmill runs were reported as interval work, and the AI saw indoor quality at HR 132-150 when it was really 158-165. Indoor work laps are now selected by HR against the athlete's threshold (with a max-HR fallback, and nothing reported when neither is set), with a minimum lap duration so an HR-lagged recovery jog cannot pass as a rep. Indoor efforts never carry a pace value at all. (Hytte-sjlwh)
- **Longest-run durability fact no longer quotes a treadmill distance** - Indoor runs correctly count towards durability because time on feet is real wherever it is run, but the fact line also printed the distance, which indoors comes from the same cadence artifact the predictor discards everywhere else. Indoor longest runs now report duration only. (Hytte-sjlwh)
