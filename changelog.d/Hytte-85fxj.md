category: Fixed
- **Transit departures now disappear once their leave time passes** - Rows used to sit at "0 min" — or dimmed as "Missed" when a walking offset was configured — for up to 30 seconds until the next Entur poll, pushing the first actually catchable departure off a 375px screen. The list is now filtered against the same one-second clock that drives the countdown, so a row leaves the moment its leave time (departure minus walking offset) passes. Pruned rows are summarised in a small "N departed" line under the stop, an off-cycle refresh (rate-limited to one per 10s) kicks in when a stop's visible list gets short, and the now-unreachable "Missed" label was removed.
