category: Added
- **Exclude rack tiles from Wordfeud suggestions** - Rack tiles on the board page are now clickable. Click one to hold it back from the move finder — useful for keeping a letter for a future turn — and click it again to put it back. Excluded tiles are dimmed and struck through, a counter shows how many of your tiles are in play, and "use all tiles" restores the whole rack. The solver re-runs automatically on each toggle. Excluded tiles still count as yours in the tile tracker and the bag-empty opponent-rack deduction, and exclusions reset whenever the rack changes.
