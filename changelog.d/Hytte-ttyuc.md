category: Changed
- **VO2max card reports a median and range instead of a trend from noisy estimates** - The training trends VO2max card now shows the median and min-to-max range of the recent per-workout estimates, and suppresses the trend label when their spread is wide enough to be estimator noise (terrain, heat, HR artefacts, pacing) rather than fitness change. The card and the race prediction on the same page summarise the same 12 most recent estimates, so they can no longer disagree about the median, the range or whether the estimates are noise. The API returns a `summary` object and a `noisy` trend value alongside the existing history. (Hytte-ttyuc)
