category: Changed
- **VO2max card reports a median and range instead of a trend from noisy estimates** - The training trends VO2max card now shows the median and min-to-max range of the recent per-workout estimates, and suppresses the trend label when their spread is wide enough to be estimator noise (terrain, heat, HR artefacts, pacing) rather than fitness change. The API returns a `summary` object and a `noisy` trend value alongside the existing history. (Hytte-ttyuc)
