- **Race predictor no longer reads a trend out of VO2max noise** - The prediction facts used to list every per-workout VO2max estimate as a "history (newest first)" sequence — mislabelled (the values were actually oldest first) and, with swings like 37 to 56, pure estimator scatter that invited the model to read a slope that was not there, while the single latest value tugged predictions away from the measured sustained-effort evidence. The estimates are now summarised as a median with an explicit spread and date range, plus a note telling the model how much to discount them when the spread is wide. (Hytte-sl97c)
