- **Push notifications for new watchlist offer matches** - Users with the offers feature, the `offers_notify` preference enabled, a watchlist and a push subscription get one push such as "3 new matches: melk, kaffe, laks" linking to /offers. It covers offers that first appeared in the last 24 hours. A background pass runs every 15 minutes and right after the daily sync, so matches held back by quiet hours or a failed send arrive once the window ends. Matching uses the same compound-word rules as the offers page, offers are never pushed twice, push endpoints are kept out of the logs, and notification failures never affect the sync or slow down an admin refresh.
