- **Stride remembers your treadmill calibration** - A new Treadmill Calibration setting stores your own measured belt-versus-outdoor speed offset and indoor heart-rate offset, and Stride now treats it as authoritative in weekly plan generation, chat, and nightly evaluations instead of re-deriving the numbers from scratch every week. (Hytte-jolsq)
- **Stride no longer invents a treadmill watch under-read percentage** - The coaching prompts stated a fixed 5-15% watch-versus-belt error, which let the coach assert confident figures the athlete's own data contradicted. Indoor watch pace is cadence-driven, so the prompts now treat it as unusable rather than as a number to correct, and an outdoor heart-rate ceiling is no longer applied unchanged indoors. (Hytte-jolsq)
