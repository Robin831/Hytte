- **Reasons when rejecting chore completions** - Rejecting a pending chore (one at a time or with "Reject selected") now opens a dialog with quick-pick reasons ("Not finished", "Do it again properly", "Wrong day") and an optional free-text field; one shared reason applies to every selected completion. Kids see the reason next to "Rejected — try again" on the chore card. The reason is stored encrypted in its own field, so the kid's own completion note is never shown as the parent's reason.
