- **Livestream from your phone** - New Live page (feature `livestream`): tap "Go live" in a phone browser to stream camera + mic over mobile data, and family members with the feature watch it live in Hytte (WebRTC, ~1–2 s delay, with automatic HLS fallback). Keeps the screen awake, reconnects after coverage drops, supports camera switch, mic mute and a low-data mode, and shows bitrate, data used and viewer count. Media runs through a localhost-only MediaMTX server that Hytte proxies behind login; stream titles are encrypted and nothing is recorded. (Hytte-krwxy)
