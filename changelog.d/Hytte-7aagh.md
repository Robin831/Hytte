category: Changed
- **Race predictions are now AI-set weekly snapshots based on facts over time** - The old prediction extrapolated Riegel from the single fastest-average-pace workout of the last 3 months (short blasts, downhill runs and treadmill sessions could set your marathon time). Predictions are now stored weekly snapshots grounded in best sustained lap-window efforts (outdoor running, net-descent excluded), the VO2max trend, twelve weeks of training load with ACR, and actual race results — AI-weighed with per-distance confidence and a rationale, clamped to a deterministic Riegel envelope so the number always stays anchored to measured efforts. The snapshot refreshes in the Monday cron right before the weekly Stride plan generates and is injected into the coach prompt, so the coach paces sessions off the same honest estimate the athlete sees. The predictions card (Trends, workout detail, and now the Stride page) shows the as-of date, change since last week, confidence, and the rationale; POST /api/training/predictions/refresh triggers an on-demand refresh.
