- **Treadmill coaching no longer invents a watch under-read figure or applies outdoor HR caps indoors** - The plan-generation and workout-evaluation prompts both claimed the watch under-reads the belt by "5-15%", inviting the coach to quote a fabricated number; the indoor pace error is cadence-driven and varies with speed, so the coach is now told never to quote a percentage and to derive the offset from the athlete's own paired belt-vs-watch data (or say the magnitude is unknown). The generation prompt also claimed the HR cap governs both indoors and out, which forced the belt down to a non-training speed — outdoor-derived zone ceilings no longer transfer to continuous indoor runs (governed instead by drift, an absolute max ceiling and nasal breathing, with a ~3-8 bpm allowance) while interval work-rep ceilings still apply unchanged. Both prompts gained the HR-curve shape diagnostic (early step-up then plateau = heat, continuous rise = too hard), and the evaluation prompt no longer scores an indoor continuous run non-compliant just for sitting a few bpm over an outdoor cap.
